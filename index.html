Forecast

AI/NA is a project that explores a dialogue between sound and movement through material collaboration. Starting in 2025, Luisa Ji works with borosilicate glass to create vessels, wearables, and sculptural mobiles that evoke a flickering balance between distraction and obsession. Her work pays homage to optical lenses, grotesque pearls, and sedimentary deposits, drawing viewers into a gaze fixed on the infinite unfolding details and the pain of a sharp reflection of light piercing into the eyes.
For the upcoming season, Luisa Ji, Dora Morgacheva (musician and sound artist), and Benjamin Lappalainen (creative technologist) continue their ongoing dialogue, conversing through a cryptic language rooted in material presence, speculative soundscape, and the simulated nervous system of a mechanical creature—breathing, sensing, and existing across different locations.
